# Home Assistant Add-on: Homestead for farmOS

Runs [farmOS](https://farmos.org), the open-source farm and garden record
keeper, on your Home Assistant.

![Supports amd64 Architecture][amd64-shield]
![Supports aarch64 Architecture][aarch64-shield]

Keep track of what grows where and what happens to it: every plant, bed and
field on a map, with its crop and variety, and logs for plantings, pruning,
harvests and observations.

- The official farmOS release, with its own SQLite database: nothing else to
  install
- Everything (the database, settings and uploaded files) is in the add-on's
  backups
- Imports from spreadsheets (CSV), with the CSV importer module
- An API key for your own sync scripts, made by the add-on, with no password
  to share

See the **Documentation** tab for setup.
